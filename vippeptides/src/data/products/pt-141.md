PT-141, also catalogued as bremelanotide, is a cyclic heptapeptide analogue of alpha-MSH and a
non-selective agonist at melanocortin receptors.

It is used in laboratory work as a reference ligand when profiling melanocortin receptor
subtypes. Supplied lyophilised with a batch certificate of analysis.
