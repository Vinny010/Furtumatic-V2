TB-500 is a synthetic peptide corresponding to the actin-binding region of thymosin beta-4,
a naturally occurring protein present in most mammalian cell types.

It is used in laboratory settings as a reference material in actin-polymerisation and
cell-motility assays. Reconstitute with bacteriostatic or sterile water immediately before use
and keep the working solution refrigerated.
