GHK-Cu is a naturally occurring tripeptide-copper(II) complex first isolated from human plasma.
Its characteristic deep blue colour comes from the coordinated copper ion.

It is one of the most extensively studied actives in topical formulation research and is
commonly used at low concentrations in stability and delivery-system experiments. Note that
the copper complex is light-sensitive and degrades in the presence of strong reducing agents.
