Epithalon (also written Epitalon) is a synthetic tetrapeptide whose sequence was derived from
epithalamin, a pineal gland extract characterised in Russian research literature.

Its short sequence makes it inexpensive to synthesise to high purity and it is frequently used
as a model substrate in peptide-stability and cell-culture longevity studies.
