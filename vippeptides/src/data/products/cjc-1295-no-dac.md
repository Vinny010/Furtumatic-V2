CJC-1295 without DAC, also catalogued as Modified GRF (1-29), is a 30-amino-acid analogue of
growth hormone releasing hormone carrying four amino acid substitutions that increase its
stability relative to the native sequence.

The absence of the Drug Affinity Complex distinguishes it from the DAC variant and gives it a
markedly shorter half-life in vitro — the reason both variants are often run side by side in
comparative assays.
