Sterile water for injection containing 0.9% benzyl alcohol as a bacteriostatic preservative,
supplied in a 10ml multi-dose vial with a rubber septum.

The preservative allows the vial to be punctured multiple times over a limited period, which is
why it is the usual choice for reconstituting lyophilised research peptides that will be
sampled more than once.
